# Acme: Messaging Guide

## Positioning Statement

For mid-market distributors who run out of the wrong stock, Acme Forecast gives buyers a weekly reorder list they trust, live in 14 days.

## Brand Voice

| Sounds Like | Doesn't Sound Like |
|---|---|
| "Your buyers approve the list. Acme does the math." | "Leverage AI-driven demand sensing." |
| "Live in 14 days." | "Rapid time to value." |

## Messaging by Audience

### VP of Operations

**Who they are:** Owns inventory value and fill rate across all warehouses.
**Primary value proposition:** Fewer stockouts without raising inventory value.
**Key messages:** A regional food distributor cut stockouts 31% with flat inventory (Strong). Live in 14 days (Moderate).
**Language to use:** "fill rate", "inventory value", "per warehouse".
**Language to avoid:** "AI", "machine learning".
**Where to deploy:** Homepage, pricing page.

### Purchasing Director

**Who they are:** Runs the buying team and its weekly reorder routine.
**Primary value proposition:** A reorder list buyers approve in minutes.
**Key messages:** Plain-language approval queue. No model tuning.
**Language to use:** "reorder list", "approve".
**Language to avoid:** "planner", "algorithm".
**Where to deploy:** Product page, demo request page.

## Message Hierarchy

1. Fewer stockouts without more stock.
2. Live in 14 days.
3. Built for buyers, not data scientists.

## Proof Points Library

- **Strong:** Regional food distributor, stockouts down 31% with flat inventory. Resonates with the VP of Operations.
- **Moderate:** Median 11 days to first forecast. Review average 4.6 of 5. Resonate with both personas.
- **Weak:** Unnamed customer quote on buyer trust. Purchasing Director.
