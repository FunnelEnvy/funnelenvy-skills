# Acme Company Facts

## Company Overview

Acme sells Acme Forecast, a demand-forecasting application for wholesale distributors with 50 to 500 employees. It connects to the distributor's existing ERP and produces weekly reorder recommendations per SKU and warehouse. Founded in 2017, about 120 employees.

## Services and Capabilities

- Weekly SKU-level demand forecasts per warehouse.
- Reorder recommendations with a buyer approval queue.
- Prebuilt connectors for three mid-market ERP systems, installed in under two weeks.
- Onboarding by a named implementation lead.

## Stated Differentiators

1. "Live in 14 days": connector install and first forecast within two weeks.
2. "Built for buyers, not data scientists": approval queue in plain language, no model tuning.
3. "Fewer stockouts without more stock": forecasts tuned to cut stockouts while holding inventory flat.
