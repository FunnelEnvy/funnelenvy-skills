# Acme Strategy Context

## Category

Acme calls its category "demand planning for distributors". Buyers search for "inventory forecasting software" and "distributor reorder software".

## Target Segments

Mid-market wholesale distributors, 50 to 500 employees, 2 to 20 warehouses. The economic buyer is the VP of Operations. The daily user is the purchasing team.

## Category Gap

Enterprise suites serve large distributors with long implementations. ERP add-on modules are cheap but generic. No vendor owns "fast-to-value forecasting for the mid-market distributor".

## Constraints

- Acme supports three ERP systems only. Distributors on other systems are out of reach.
- No published security certification.
