# Phase 4 -- Self-Critique Grading

Goal: grade each item's own `Self-critique` block on whether it genuinely rebuts the objection it raises, or only deflects it. This phase runs AFTER the checklist (Phase 3), because a self-critique deflects precisely when it waves away a cross-cutting finding that applies to its item, and you can only see that once the checklist findings exist.

Skip this phase entirely if the roadmap carries no `Self-critique` blocks (pre-v1.5.0 output); note the omission in the critique header.

## The grade

For each item that has a `Self-critique`:

- **Grade: Rebut / Partial / Deflect.**
  - **Rebut** -- the self-critique raises the real objection AND resolves it with cited evidence or a sound design change.
  - **Partial** -- it raises the objection but resolves it with an unverified assertion, or it raises some objections but misses the checklist finding that applies to its item.
  - **Deflect** -- it raises the objection and waves it away with an unsupported claim, or it names the objection to look thorough while changing nothing.
- **Cite the specific rebuttal sentence.** Quote the sentence in the self-critique that does (or fails to do) the rebutting. A grade with no quoted sentence is not a finding.
- **Name the checklist finding it failed to confront**, if any. Tie the grade to the Phase 3 output: "the self-critique does not confront CC3 (the headline number is uncorrected for the caveat the source documents)."
- **Second-order miss.** Note any objection the self-critique never raises at all that the Phase 2 skeptic or Phase 3 checklist did (a failure mode the author did not see, not just one they deflected).

## The point of this phase

The reference pass found that self-critiques reliably end in a confident rebuttal and almost never concede. This phase makes that pattern visible and specific: it is not enough to say "the self-critiques are weak"; the grade cites the exact sentence and the exact unconfronted finding. That specificity is what makes the critique actionable and what distinguishes this skill's output from the roadmap's own self-critique.

## Output of this phase (held in-session)

Per item with a self-critique: the grade (Rebut / Partial / Deflect), the quoted rebuttal sentence, the unconfronted checklist finding(s), and any second-order miss. Feeds the per-item section of the rendered critique.
